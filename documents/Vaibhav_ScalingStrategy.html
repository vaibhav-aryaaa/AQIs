The Problem & AI Solution

01

⚠️ The Core Problem

  • Centralized Inefficiencies: Continuous monitoring stations (CAAQMS) are capital-heavy (₹1.5–2M each), resulting in sparse grids and massive spatial blind spots.
  • Raw Unvalidated Streams: IoT sensors suffer from drift and errors. An Ahmedabad station recorded an impossible AQI of 2049.0, skewing municipal emergency actions.
  • Reactive Logging: Current platforms tell us what happened yesterday; they do not forecast tomorrow's air quality drop.

⚡ The SmartAQI Solution

  • Distributed Micro-Grid: Dense array of low-cost IoT nodes capturing localized street-level $PM_{2.5}$ and $CO$ values.
  • Spatial Anomaly Quarantine: Backend validator dynamically compares telemetry to neighbors within 2 km, blocking and logging outliers.
  • 24-Hour Predictive Lead: XGBoost ML engine maps local telemetry and meteorological variables to forecast tomorrow's localized AQI in milliseconds.

Target Users & Value Proposition

02

👥 Asthmatics & Citizens

  • Who: Vulnerable urban populations, outdoor laborers, and school children exposed to toxic air during winter inversions.
  • Value: Telegram bot pushes instant, localized health advisories 24h in advance, allowing them to shift schedules and minimize respiratory strain.

🏢 Municipal Authorities

  • Who: Environmental Boards and Sanitation Departments coordinating pollution mitigation.
  • Value: 10x spatial density at <5% of the cost. The app replaces blanket city lockdowns with targeted GRAP action tickets (e.g., mist-sprinkling only in hotspots).

🚦 Traffic & Transit Planners

  • Who: Urban traffic controllers directing vehicle streams during high-pollution rush hours.
  • Value: Hyper-local coordinates map bottlenecks, triggering dynamic traffic diversions around highly congested residential buffers.

Risks & Mitigation

05

🛠️ Hardware Durability

  • Challenge: Cheap optical sensors degrade quickly under heavy soot, dust, and humidity, causing drift.
  • Mitigation: Implement automated relative calibration scripts comparing node baselines with nearest reference CAAQMS. Use a local host partnership model.

🔌 Network / API Losses

  • Challenge: Local Wi-Fi drops or Open-Meteo connection failure blocks predictive calculations.
  • Mitigation: Integrate a local baseline lookup database (coded as offline fallbacks in `predictor.py`) utilizing historical weather means for calculation backup.

🔔 False Alerts & Panic

  • Challenge: A faulty reading slipping through checks can trigger false alarms, causing public panic or unnecessary shutdowns.
  • Mitigation: Utilize the two-tier filter: absolute value checks combined with spatial consistency verification ($3\sigma$ neighbors check) before broadcasting.
